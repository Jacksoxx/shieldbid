How a bid works

closed round — kept as the record
DEMO-01 is closed and settled. No round is open right now, so nothing can be bid at the address at the bottom of this card. When the next lot opens, its UA and deadline are published here first.
  1. Open a wallet with a shielded balance

    Zodl, Ywallet, Edge, Zkool, Zingo! or Noir. A shielded send is the only requirement — no memo needed.

  2. Send to the seller's published UA — the amount is your bid

    The amount is encrypted by the shielded pool: the public and every other bidder see only that some transfer happened. Set it to the maximum price you are willing to pay — you pay that only if you win, and if the clearing price is lower the difference comes back to the address you sent from.

  3. A memo is optional decoration only

    If your wallet has a memo field you may type something readable for yourself — the protocol never parses it, the amount carries the bid:

What this does not do

stated, not hidden

The seller is a trusted party. It holds the lot keys, so it can decrypt a bid from the first block, and it can lie about the arithmetic behind the published clearing price. A third party cannot recompute that price from chain data alone — that is the price of privacy. What the chain does prove is the money: every revealed leg must be backed by a real txid paying the lot address.

No live “current high bid” exists. The amounts are encrypted, so nobody — not even the seller — can honestly publish a standing price during the round. We do not fake one.

A sent shielded transfer is final. A bid cannot be retracted after the deadline, so bid a price you are happy to pay. Custody is the seller's until the refund batch is broadcast — its txids are published above.

The asset is out of scope. Zcash has no live mainnet asset/NFT standard, so the lot's item is referenced off-chain by id/URI, and no item changed hands in DEMO-01. ShieldBid settles the price discovery, not the asset.

Before you send

  • ✓Zodl, Ywallet, Edge — desktop & mobile, shielded send built in
  • ✓Zkool, Zingo!, Nighthawk, Noir — community wallets, shielded send built in
  • ✓Any wallet that can pay a u1 (unified) address — that is the only requirement
  • ✗Exchange withdrawals — exchanges hold ZEC transparently, so the payout lands on a t1 and the amount is public
  • ✗Multi-coin & hardware wallets — if the app has no shielded (u1) send of its own, a bid is impossible from it
  • ✗Transparent (t-addr) sends — the amount is public on chain
Self-test: open your wallet's send screen. If it offers no shielded/private send (no u1 recipient), it cannot bid — shield your ZEC into a shielded-capable wallet first.
Bids sent before the window opens, after the deadline, or below the reserve are not counted. A late transfer is not refunded automatically — the seller returns it in the final batch.
