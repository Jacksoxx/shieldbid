5 / Honest limits

What ShieldBid does not do

  • No live high bidThe amounts are encrypted, so nobody — not even the seller — can honestly compute a running highest bid. Bidders decide on their own valuation, not on a leaderboard.
  • Trusted sellerThe seller decides the clearing price and the ranking, and a third party cannot verify those from the chain alone. What the chain does prove is the money.
  • No withdrawalsA bid cannot be retracted — the transfer is already on-chain. Funds are returned after the reveal, so a bidder must be sure before sending.
